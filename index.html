Nahgoo is built around real places around you — finding them, walking to them, and checking in once you're actually there. It needs your location for that core loop to work.

Capture places you reach

When you arrive at a place, you capture it through the camera — and every capture is logged as a stamp in your passport. Enable the camera so you're ready the moment you get there.

Collect the places near you

Walk to real spots around you and capture them to earn passport stamps. Find somewhere worth sharing? Submit it as a gem so others can discover it too.

Delete account

This cannot be undone.

Permanently deletes your Nahgoo account and erases everything tied to it — your captures and the places they were made at, your travel distance, every stamp, and every guide you've made or saved. Everywhere, not just on this phone. This cannot be undone.

Two things this does not remove. Gems you've submitted stay on the map — once approved they're public spots other people use — but they're unlinked from you, so nothing shows you as their submitter. And guide codes you've already shared keep working for the people you sent them to.

About this app

Your data

Nahgoo doesn't store your location. Your position is used in the moment — to draw the map and measure how far a pin is — then it's gone.

Read the full privacy policy — exactly what's kept in your account, every service that receives anything, that your display name is public, and how to have it all erased.
