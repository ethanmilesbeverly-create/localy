# Nothing within 250 m; the name scan found no observatory pin within 1.5 km (the Allegheny Observatory is a different one, ~2 km south).
First Aluminum Observatory Dome | @40.499846,-80.012819 | On the hill just west of here in 1930, Pittsburgh amateur astronomers led by Leo J. Scanlon built the Valley View Observatory, the first known observatory topped with an aluminum dome, and its shiny shell became the model for how a modern observatory is pictured. | https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=35633
