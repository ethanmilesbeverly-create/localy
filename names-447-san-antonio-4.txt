Eagar House | @29.419444,-98.488056 | This six-room stone house, built at the end of the Civil War, was the home of Sarah Eagar — born in 1842, the first Anglo-American girl born in San Antonio — whose father gave her the land, then bordered by the Alamo acequia, as a wedding present. | https://www.txgenwebcounties.com/bexar/Landmarks/Eagar_House.htm | new
Emil Elmendorf House | @29.432778,-98.472778 | Businessman and civic leader Emil Elmendorf, part owner of the big hardware firm Elmendorf and Co., had Alfred Giles design this house in 1884; mixing Greek Revival and Victorian styling, it is one of the city's few remaining raised cottages, with the main floor set above an aboveground basement. | https://www.txgenwebcounties.com/bexar/Landmarks/Elmendorf,_Emil_House.htm
